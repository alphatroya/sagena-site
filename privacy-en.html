Privacy Policy

Sagena · BlinkPeak OU · Effective 12 October 2026

РусскийEnglish

Sagena keeps your logbook and settings on your device and passes on almost nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no ad networks. A single network request checks the time of a new logbook entry with our server; the device's advertising identifier is sent in that request only if you allowed tracking.

Information we collect

When you save a night's result to the logbook, the game asks our server (api.platform-core.com) for the current time so the entry can be marked as verified. Before the first such request iOS asks for tracking permission:

  • If you allow it, the request also carries the device's advertising identifier (IDFA). We may use it to show advertising. The identifier is not linked to your name, account or any other data in the game.
  • If you decline, the game does not read or send the advertising identifier. The entry is saved with the device clock and the game works in full.

The server may be unreachable or may answer without a time — then the entry again keeps the device time and no verification mark appears. Like any network request, contacting the server reveals your device's IP address. Your photos, voice notes, text, coordinates and logbook entries are not part of the request and never leave the device. Apart from this request we neither receive nor store any information about you.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following locally, inside its own app container:

  • Settings: sound, haptics and the daily reminder to set out.
  • Records: your best night and your largest catch.
  • A flag that the intro briefing has been shown.
  • The logbook: entry date, night number, catch weight, whether the night was a success, a text note, a cover photo, a voice note, coordinates and place name, and whether the entry time was verified with our server.

Photos and voice notes are stored as files inside the app sandbox. None of this is uploaded. Deleting the app erases it permanently. Catch cards you saved to Photos yourself stay in your library — delete them there.

Device permissions

Every permission is optional, is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and declining leaves the rest of the game fully working.

  • Notifications — to remind you once a day to set out, if you enable the reminder in settings. If you decline, the reminder does not arrive and the game goes on.
  • Camera — to photograph your catch and use it as a logbook cover. The photo stays on the device. If you decline, the entry is saved without a cover.
  • Microphone — to record and play back a voice note for a logbook entry. The recording stays on the device. If you decline, you can skip the note.
  • Photo access (read) — to pick an existing photo as a logbook cover. The game copies the chosen image; the rest of your library is not read. If you decline, you can use the camera or go without a cover.
  • Photo access (add) — to save a catch card to Photos when you ask for it. If you decline, the card cannot be saved and the game goes on.
  • Location (when in use) — to mark where a night trip took place and put it on the catch card. Coordinates stay on the device. If you decline, the entry is saved without a place.
  • Tracking — to send the advertising identifier with the time-check request, as described above. If you decline, the identifier is not sent.

The night-water loop keeps playing while the app is in the background or the screen is off. That is not a separate permission and collects no data. Links such as sagena:// just open a screen inside the game and send no data out.

Network requests and third parties

The only address the game contacts is our own server api.platform-core.com, for the utils.getServerTime check. We use no third-party SDKs, ad networks or analytics systems. Without the internet, when the server is blocked, or when tracking is denied, the game works fully offline and entries are saved with the device clock.

Children

The game collects no personal data and is not aimed at collecting data about children. We do not ask for a name, email, phone number or age, and there are no accounts.

Your rights

Everything about your play is stored on your device, so you control it directly: deleting the app erases settings, records and the logbook. Permissions can be changed at any time in iOS settings. For questions about the advertising identifier we transmit, write to the address below.
